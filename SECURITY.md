# Security policy

Seki is pre-release research and engineering work. No implementation or
generated artifact is currently qualified for security- or authority-critical
production use.

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through the repository's
security-reporting channel or private security advisory facility. Do not open a
public issue for an unpatched vulnerability. Include affected revisions,
reproduction steps, impact, and any proposed mitigation.

Security reports do not themselves establish proof failure or proof validity.
Reports affecting a semantic, representation, compiler, certificate, or
toolchain boundary must identify that boundary explicitly.
