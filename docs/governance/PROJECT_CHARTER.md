# Project charter

Seki is an independent project implementing the language identity
`io.frogfish.seki/language@0`.

Gnosis and Kiku jointly specified the initial charter from their shared need
for small decision kernels whose generated C implementations can be connected
to formal semantics. They are founding customers and valued reviewers. They
have no special authority over Seki's governance, semantics, implementation,
qualification, or releases beyond the influence available to ordinary
customers and contributors.

Customer-specific concepts belong in versioned libraries, examples, adapters,
and qualification verticals. They must not enter the language core merely to
satisfy one customer.

The frozen v0.3 seed remains the governing design input until a later revision
is adopted by this project. Qualification claims are controlled by
`PROJECT_STATUS.json` and the F0–F8 ladder in the seed specification.
