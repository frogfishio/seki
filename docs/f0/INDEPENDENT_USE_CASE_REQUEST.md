# Independent F0 use-case request

Status: seeking a materially different bounded decision

Seki already has Gnosis and Kiku as founding customers. Global F0 nevertheless
requires a use case materially different from the Kiku Arena causal-provider
decision. The purpose is to test the generality of the language charter, not to
grant the reviewing customer governance authority.

Gnosis, SIRCC, Tenkan, or another consumer may propose the case. A useful
submission is one or two pages and answers:

1. What consequential decision does the kernel make?
2. What are its immutable inputs and result?
3. Which identities must remain nominally distinct?
4. What are the maximum collection, traversal, step, stack, and workspace
   bounds?
5. Which malformed, missing, duplicate, ambiguous, stale, substituted, or
   exhausted cases must be rejected, and in what precedence?
6. Which core operations are required?
7. Can the decision be expressed without Kiku, Arena, GCIR, or
   customer-specific primitives in the language core?
8. Could the consumer eventually supply the independent F7 vertical?

Promising shapes include bounded control-flow validation, deterministic
source/target correspondence, protocol-state admission, and capability-policy
evaluation. The F0 response is a charter review, not an implementation or
product acceptance.
